# Tribal Governance Declaration

**System:** Codex of the Living — Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation Infrastructure
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-06
**Governing Authority:** Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (VBTN)

---

## 1. Governing Instruments

This system operates exclusively under the governance authority of the **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (VBTN)**. The following instruments govern its operation, modification, and disposition:

| Instrument | Document | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Tribal Sovereignty Declaration | `TribalSovereigntyDeclaration.pdf` | Foundational |
| Bylaws of Verdigris Botanica Estate | `BylawsofVerdigrisBotanicaEstate.pdf` | Operational |
| Tribal Membership Agreement | `TribalMembershipAgreement.pdf` | Membership |
| TRV Master Trust and Funding Agreement | `TRVMasterTrustAndFundingAgreement.pdf` | Financial |
| Trust Indenture | `TrustIndenture.pdf` | Asset Holding |
| Infrastructure and Service Agreement | `InfrastructureandServiceAgreement.pdf` | Technical |
| Internal Policies and Procedures Manual | `InternalPoliciesandProceduresManualcomplete.pdf` | Operational |

Any version updates to these instruments that materially affect this system require a documented Tribal Resolution and must be reflected in this file.

---

## 2. Primacy of Tribal Law

No vendor agreement, cloud service contract, federal procurement instrument, or third-party integration supersedes the governing authority of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation over this system. Specifically:

- **Microsoft Azure / M365** hosting and identity services are engaged as infrastructure providers only; they hold no governance authority over data, operations, or decisions.
- **DocuSign** is engaged as a document execution platform only; document content, legal effect, and ownership remain with VBTN.
- Any future integrators or vendors are subject to the same constraint.

---

## 3. Jurisdictional Primacy

The primary jurisdiction over this system is the **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation**. Federal frameworks applicable to tribal operations (including but not limited to 2 CFR 200 and applicable FAR provisions) apply as required by law but do not displace tribal sovereignty.

See [`JURISDICTION.md`](JURISDICTION.md) for the full jurisdictional statement.

---

## 4. No Unilateral Transfer

This system — including its code, data, integrations, schemas, and documentation — may not be transferred, sold, licensed, assigned, or placed under the authority of any external party without:

1. A formal Tribal Resolution adopted by a quorum of the VBTN governing body, **and**
2. Review and approval by the System Steward and Elder Council seat as defined in [`docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md).

---

## 5. Succession

Authority over this system does not belong to any single individual. Governance succession is defined in [`docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md). In the event of incapacity or absence of the current System Steward, authority passes immediately to the Tribal Technical Lead, with Elder Council oversight.

---

## 6. Anti-Weaponization Commitment

The VBTN hereby declares that this system shall never be used as a tool of surveillance, behavioral scoring, predictive profiling, or exploitation against any member of the Nation or any community it serves. This commitment is embedded in code (see `app/config.py`), contracts (see `legal/`), and governance (see `docs/PROHIBITED_USES.md`).
