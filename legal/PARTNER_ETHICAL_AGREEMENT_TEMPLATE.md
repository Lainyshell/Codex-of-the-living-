## Recitals

VBTN has built the Codex of the Living to serve its members and Nation with dignity, sovereignty, and safety. The integrity of this system depends on every party who touches it committing to use it — and the data within it — only for its intended, beneficial purposes.

This Addendum defines the ethical boundaries that apply to all Partners.

---

## Article 1 — Absolute Prohibitions

Partner irrevocably agrees that it shall **never** use this system, its data, or its outputs — in whole or in part — for any of the following purposes:

### 1.1 Surveillance
- Monitoring, tracking, or profiling VBTN members or their activities without explicit, documented tribal authorization
- Creating records of member movements, associations, communications, or behaviors beyond what is strictly necessary for the authorized service
- Enabling or assisting any government agency, law enforcement, or third party in surveilling VBTN members

### 1.2 Behavioral Scoring and Predictive Profiling
- Building or contributing to systems that score individuals based on predicted future behavior
- Creating risk profiles, creditworthiness assessments, or "threat scores" using VBTN data
- Training machine learning models on VBTN member data without explicit tribal authorization

### 1.3 Data Brokering
- Selling, sharing, trading, or licensing VBTN data to any third party
- Incorporating VBTN data into commercial data products or data marketplaces
- Allowing VBTN data to be used as part of any data exchange or data cooperative

### 1.4 Discrimination and Targeting
- Using VBTN data to discriminate against any individual in housing, employment, credit, insurance, or public accommodation
- Using VBTN data to target individuals for adverse treatment based on race, tribal membership, religion, national origin, or any protected characteristic
- Enabling any form of predatory targeting of VBTN members

### 1.5 Exploitation
- Using VBTN data to financially exploit members or the Nation
- Structuring services in ways designed to create dependency or extract value from VBTN
- Using privileged access to VBTN systems to benefit Partner at VBTN's expense

### 1.6 Weaponization
- Assisting any party — including government agencies — in using VBTN data against the interests of the Nation or its members
- Providing access to VBTN systems or data in response to informal requests (as opposed to valid legal process with notice to VBTN)
- Taking any action that would place system capabilities in conflict with VBTN's sovereignty

---

## Article 2 — Affirmative Obligations

Partner agrees to:

**2.1** Use VBTN system access and data only for the specific, documented purpose described in the base agreement.

**2.2** Immediately notify VBTN upon discovering any use of VBTN data that may violate this Addendum, whether by Partner's own personnel or any subcontractor.

**2.3** Train all personnel with access to VBTN systems on these prohibitions before granting access.

**2.4** Maintain records demonstrating compliance with this Addendum for three years and provide them to VBTN upon request.

**2.5** Flow down these obligations to any subcontractor or sub-processor who handles VBTN data.

---

## Article 3 — Breach and Consequences

**3.1 Material Breach:** Any violation of Article 1 constitutes an automatic, incurable material breach of the base agreement.

**3.2 Immediate Termination:** Upon discovery of any Article 1 violation, VBTN may immediately terminate the base agreement without notice, cure period, or liability.

**3.3 Data Return:** Upon termination for breach, Partner must return all VBTN data within 7 days and delete all copies within 14 days, with written certification.

**3.4 Remedies:** VBTN reserves all remedies available under tribal law and applicable federal law, including injunctive relief without bond and damages.

**3.5 Survival:** This Addendum survives termination of the base agreement with respect to data already in Partner's possession.

---

## Article 4 — Audit Rights

VBTN reserves the right to audit Partner's compliance with this Addendum upon 30 days' written notice. Partner shall provide reasonable access to records and personnel for this purpose.
