## Non-Transferability of System Assets
## Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation — Codex of the Living System

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-06

---

## 1. Statement of Non-Transferability

The Codex of the Living system — including its source code, data, documentation, integrations, schemas, governance instruments, intellectual property, and all associated assets (collectively, the "System Assets") — is held in trust by and for the **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (VBTN)**.

**No person, entity, or authority may sell, transfer, assign, license, sublicense, pledge, encumber, donate, bequeath, or otherwise dispose of any System Asset without:**

1. A formal **Tribal Resolution** adopted by a quorum of the VBTN governing body, **and**
2. Written approval by the **System Steward** and **Elder Council Seat** as defined in [`../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md), **and**
3. Review by qualified legal counsel to confirm the transfer is consistent with tribal law and applicable federal law.

---

## 2. What Cannot Be Transferred Without Tribal Resolution

The following specific actions are prohibited without the above approvals:

| Action | Prohibited Without Resolution |
|---|---|
| Selling the System or any component | ✅ Prohibited |
| Licensing the System to any third party | ✅ Prohibited |
| Assigning IP rights to any party other than VBTN Trust | ✅ Prohibited |
| Forking and operating under different governance | ✅ Prohibited |
| Moving the System to a vendor outside VBTN's control | ✅ Prohibited |
| Pledging System Assets as collateral | ✅ Prohibited |
| Merging or integrating the System into another legal entity | ✅ Prohibited |
| Transferring administrative control to any individual not designated by Tribal Resolution | ✅ Prohibited |

---

## 3. Death, Incapacity, or Departure of System Steward

System Assets do **not** pass to any individual's estate, heirs, assigns, or creditors upon the death or incapacity of the System Steward or any contributor. All rights vest exclusively in and remain with the VBTN Trust.

The succession process defined in [`../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md) Section 5 governs operational continuity.

---

## 4. Contractor and Vendor Non-Transferability

Any contractor, vendor, or integrator who contributes to the System must:

1. Assign all intellectual property created for the System to the VBTN Trust (see [`IP_ASSIGNMENT.md`](IP_ASSIGNMENT.md))
2. Acknowledge that they acquire no ownership interest in the System
3. Agree that they may not use, copy, or transfer System Assets after their engagement ends

---

## 5. Enforcement

Any purported transfer, sale, assignment, or license made in violation of this clause is void and of no legal effect. VBTN reserves all remedies available under tribal law and applicable federal law.
