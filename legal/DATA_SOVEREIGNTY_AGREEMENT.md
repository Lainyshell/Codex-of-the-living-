## Article 1 — Declaration of Data Sovereignty

The **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation** hereby declares and affirms:

**1.1** All data created, collected, processed, stored, transmitted, or derived through the Codex of the Living system (the "System") is and remains the exclusive property of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation.

**1.2** No vendor, cloud provider, integrator, contractor, partner, or third party acquires any ownership, license, or proprietary interest in VBTN data by virtue of providing services to or in connection with the System.

**1.3** VBTN's data sovereignty is a manifestation of its inherent tribal sovereignty and is not contingent upon any vendor contract or federal recognition.

---

## Article 2 — Scope of Data Covered

This agreement covers all data processed by the System, including:

- Member identity and contact information
- Financial records, ledgers, and transaction data
- Documents executed through DocuSign or any other platform
- Trust and estate records
- Governance and meeting records
- Technical metadata and system logs
- Any data derived, aggregated, or inferred from the above

---

## Article 3 — Vendor Obligations

Any vendor, cloud provider, integrator, or contractor who processes VBTN data pursuant to the System agrees and acknowledges:

**3.1 No Ownership:** Vendor acquires no ownership, license, or residual rights in VBTN data.

**3.2 Purpose Limitation:** Vendor may process VBTN data only for the specific, documented purpose for which it was engaged. No secondary use, analytics, training, or repurposing of VBTN data is permitted.

**3.3 No Sale or Transfer:** Vendor shall not sell, license, assign, share, or transfer VBTN data to any third party without a written Tribal Resolution authorizing such transfer.

**3.4 Return and Deletion:** Upon termination of the vendor relationship, vendor shall:
- Return all VBTN data in a portable, open format (JSON, CSV, or equivalent) within 30 days
- Permanently delete all copies of VBTN data from vendor systems within 60 days
- Provide written certification of deletion

**3.5 Compelled Disclosure:** If vendor receives a legal demand (subpoena, court order, government request) for VBTN data, vendor shall:
- Immediately notify VBTN (within 24 hours where legally permissible)
- Provide VBTN a reasonable opportunity to object or seek protection
- Assert applicable tribal sovereign immunity defenses on VBTN's behalf if directed

**3.6 Security:** Vendor shall maintain reasonable security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of VBTN data and notify VBTN immediately upon discovering any breach or unauthorized access.

## Article 5 — Data Residency

All VBTN data must reside within United States sovereign cloud infrastructure (US Government regions) unless a Tribal Resolution explicitly authorizes alternative residency. No data may be transferred to foreign jurisdictions.

---

## Article 6 — Member Data Rights

Individual members of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation retain the right to:
- Know what data the System holds about them
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of their data (subject to legal retention requirements)

These rights are administered by the System Steward per the governance framework in [`../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](../docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md).

---

## Article 7 — Enforcement

Violation of this agreement by any vendor or partner constitutes material breach of their service agreement with VBTN and may result in:
- Immediate termination of the vendor relationship
- Demand for return/deletion of all data
- Legal action under tribal law and applicable federal law
