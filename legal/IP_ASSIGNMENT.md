## Recitals

WHEREAS, the Assignor(s) have created, developed, and contributed to the software system known as the **Codex of the Living**, including all associated source code, documentation, data schemas, database structures, integrations, workflows, configuration files, deployment scripts, and derivative works (collectively, the "Work");

WHEREAS, the Work was created for and on behalf of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (VBTN) and its Trust, in furtherance of the Nation's sovereign governance, financial, and operational purposes;

WHEREAS, it is the intent of the parties that all intellectual property rights in the Work vest permanently and exclusively in the Trust, for the benefit of the Nation and its members;

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Assignment

Assignor(s) hereby irrevocably and unconditionally assign to the Trust all right, title, and interest in and to the Work, including:

1. **Copyright** — all rights under 17 U.S.C. § 101 et seq. and equivalent international laws
2. **Trade secrets** — all proprietary technical knowledge embedded in the Work
3. **Moral rights** — to the extent waivable, Assignor(s) waive all moral rights in the Work
4. **Derivative works** — all rights in any future derivatives of the Work
5. **Documentation** — all technical documentation, runbooks, architecture diagrams, and governance instruments
6. **Data schemas** — all database schemas, data models, and data structures

---

## 2. Scope of the Work

The Work includes, without limitation:

- Source code in the `app/` directory and all subdirectories
- Database schema files
- GitHub Actions workflows in `.github/workflows/`
- All documentation in `docs/` and `legal/`
- All governance instruments (TRIBAL_GOVERNANCE.md, JURISDICTION.md, GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md, etc.)
- Integration configurations for Azure, DocuSign, and any future platforms
- Container configurations (Dockerfile, compose.yaml)
- Any commits to the repository `Lainyshell/Codex-of-the-living-` and its successors

---

## 3. Representations and Warranties

Assignor(s) represent and warrant that:

1. They are the sole creators of the portions of the Work they contributed, or have obtained valid assignments from any co-contributors
2. The Work does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights
3. No portion of the Work is subject to any prior assignment, encumbrance, or license that conflicts with this assignment
4. They have full authority to execute this assignment

---

## 4. Further Assurances

Assignor(s) agree to execute such additional documents and take such further actions as the Trust may reasonably request to perfect, record, or enforce the rights assigned herein.

---

## 5. Non-Transferability

The Trust's interest in the Work is non-transferable except as provided in [`NON_TRANSFERABILITY_CLAUSE.md`](NON_TRANSFERABILITY_CLAUSE.md).

---

## 6. Governing Law

This assignment is governed by the law of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation and applicable federal law. See [`JURISDICTION.md`](../JURISDICTION.md).
