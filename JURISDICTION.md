# Jurisdiction Declaration

**System:** Codex of the Living — Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation Infrastructure
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-06
**Status:** Binding on all users, operators, vendors, and integrators

---

## 1. Primary Jurisdiction

This system is owned, operated, and governed under the jurisdiction of the **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (VBTN)**, a sovereign tribal nation. Tribal law and governance instruments are the primary legal framework for all questions of system ownership, data rights, operational decisions, and dispute resolution.

---

## 2. Federal Framework Applicability

The following federal frameworks apply to this system where required by the nature of VBTN's federal relationships, grants, and procurement activities. They supplement — and do not displace — tribal jurisdiction:

| Framework | Applicability |
|---|---|
| **2 CFR Part 200** (Uniform Guidance) | Federal grant administration, cost principles, audit requirements |
| **FAR (Federal Acquisition Regulation)** | Federal procurement activities and subcontracting |
| **Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (ISDEAA), 25 U.S.C. § 5301 et seq.** | Tribal self-governance compact operations |
| **Privacy Act of 1974** | Federal agency interactions involving personally identifiable information |
| **FISMA / FedRAMP** | Security standards for federally-hosted or federally-funded systems where applicable |

---

## 3. Vendor and Cloud Provider Jurisdiction

Vendors and cloud infrastructure providers (including but not limited to Microsoft Azure US Government, DocuSign) are engaged as **service providers only**. Their terms of service govern the technical delivery of their services; they do not:

- Assert jurisdiction over VBTN data
- Acquire ownership or licensing rights to any data, schemas, or documents processed through this system
- Have authority to compel disclosure of VBTN data except as required by valid legal process directed to VBTN

---

## 4. Data Residency and Sovereignty

All data created, processed, or stored by this system:

- Is the exclusive property of the **Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation**
- Must remain within US-sovereign infrastructure (US Government cloud regions) unless a Tribal Resolution explicitly authorizes otherwise
- Is subject to VBTN's data sovereignty instruments (see [`legal/DATA_SOVEREIGNTY_AGREEMENT.md`](legal/DATA_SOVEREIGNTY_AGREEMENT.md))

---

## 5. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute concerning this system — its operation, data, contracts, or governance — shall first be submitted to the VBTN governing body for resolution under tribal law. Only where tribal jurisdiction is legally unavailable shall disputes proceed to federal court, in which case the venue shall be the federal courts with Indian Country jurisdiction applicable to VBTN's territory.

No vendor or partner agreement may specify state court jurisdiction or private arbitration as the exclusive forum for disputes affecting VBTN data or system governance without a Tribal Resolution explicitly authorizing that choice of forum.

---

This declaration is subject to the instruments listed in [`TRIBAL_GOVERNANCE.md`](TRIBAL_GOVERNANCE.md) and the governance structure defined in [`docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md`](docs/GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md).

## 7. Change Control

Amendments to this document require:

1. A Tribal Resolution from the VBTN governing body, **and**
2. A pull request approved by at least two designated stewards per [`docs/CRITICAL_CHANGE_PROTOCOL.md`](docs/CRITICAL_CHANGE_PROTOCOL.md)
