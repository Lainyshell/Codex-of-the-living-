# Sovereign Technology Trust — Proposal

**Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation**
**Document Type:** Proposal / Planning Instrument
**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Status:** Draft for Tribal Council Consideration

---

## Executive Summary

This document proposes the establishment of a **Sovereign Technology Trust** — a purpose-built legal structure whose sole mission is to hold, protect, and sustain the Codex of the Living infrastructure as a permanent asset of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation.

The Trust would ensure that no single person's absence, death, or incapacity can threaten the system's continuity — and that the system can never be captured, sold, or turned against the Nation.

---

## 1. Why a Sovereign Technology Trust?

### The Problem with Individual Ownership
When infrastructure is tied to an individual:
- Their death may trigger estate proceedings that threaten the asset
- Their incapacity leaves no clear successor
- Their personal creditors may have claims
- They can be pressured, coerced, or compromised

### The Solution
A trust holds assets for defined beneficiaries and purposes, managed by trustees under strict fiduciary obligations — and survives any individual trustee.

---

## 2. Proposed Trust Structure

### Name
**Verdigris Botanica Nation Technology Trust**
(or: "VBTN Codex Trust")

### Purpose (Proposed Mission Statement)
> To hold, protect, maintain, and develop the Codex of the Living infrastructure system as a permanent asset of the Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation, for the benefit of the Nation and its members, in perpetuity and in accordance with the Nation's sovereignty, governance instruments, and values.

### Beneficiaries
- The Verdigris Botanica Tribal Nation (primary)
- Members of the Nation (as recipients of system services)

### Trustees (Proposed — 3 minimum)
| Role | Appointment | Term |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Trustee | Tribal Resolution | 3 years, renewable |
| Technical Trustee | System Steward or designee | 2 years, renewable |
| Elder Trustee | Elder Council designation | Indefinite |

### Trustee Powers
- Hold title to all System Assets on behalf of VBTN
- Engage vendors and contractors
- Execute and enforce agreements
- Make operational decisions within governance bounds
- Cannot: sell, transfer, or encumber assets without a Tribal Resolution

---

## 3. Assets to be Held by the Trust

Upon establishment, the Trust would hold:

1. **Intellectual property** assigned per [`IP_ASSIGNMENT.md`](IP_ASSIGNMENT.md)
2. **Domain names and digital identities** (vbtn.org, related)
3. **Cloud infrastructure accounts** (Azure Government subscriptions)
4. **Data** (all member, financial, and governance data per [`DATA_SOVEREIGNTY_AGREEMENT.md`](DATA_SOVEREIGNTY_AGREEMENT.md))
5. **Contracts** with vendors and integrators
6. **Documentation and runbooks** (transferred to Trust custody)

---

## 4. Relationship to Existing Governance

The Sovereign Technology Trust would operate **within** the VBTN governance structure, not outside it:

- The Trust is subject to the **Tribal Governance Charter** ([`../TRIBAL_GOVERNANCE.md`](../TRIBAL_GOVERNANCE.md))
- Major decisions require **Tribal Resolutions** as specified in the Critical Change Protocol
- The Trust reports to the **VBTN governing body** annually
- Trust governance documents are version-controlled in this repository

---

## 5. Formation Path

### Step 1 — Tribal Resolution of Intent (30 days)
The VBTN governing body adopts a resolution declaring intent to form the Trust and directing its preparation.

### Step 2 — Trust Instrument Drafting (60 days)
Working with tribal legal counsel, draft the Trust Indenture covering:
- Trust purpose and duration (perpetual)
- Trustee appointment, powers, and removal procedures
- Successor trustee provisions
- Asset schedule
- Amendment procedures (requiring Tribal Resolution)
- Dissolution provisions (assets revert to Nation)

### Step 3 — Asset Transfer (90 days)
- IP Assignment executed by all contributors
- Vendor agreements novated to the Trust
- Cloud accounts brought under Trust ownership
- Data sovereignty agreements updated

### Step 4 — Governance Integration (120 days)
- CODEOWNERS updated to reflect Trust-held roles
- GitHub organization transferred or co-owned by Trust
- Annual reporting schedule established
